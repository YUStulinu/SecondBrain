# Northwind Labs: team handbook

(A fictional company, written as sample material for the Second Brain demo.)

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in your own time zone. Outside core hours, work when it suits you, as
long as your team knows how to reach you.

## Remote work

Everyone may work remotely up to three days a week. Fully remote arrangements need a written
agreement with your manager and are reviewed every six months.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees get 25 days of paid leave a year, plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can
be carried over to the next year; they expire on 31 March. Book leave longer than one week at least
four weeks in advance.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. From the fourth consecutive day of
sickness, a doctor's note is required.

## Expenses

Expenses up to 100 EUR need no approval beforehand. Anything above 100 EUR needs your manager's
approval before you spend it. Submit receipts within 30 days through the expenses tool; claims older
than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Equipment

Each employee gets a laptop, renewed every three years, and a one-time home-office budget of
500 EUR for a chair, a desk or a monitor.

## Security

Turn on full-disk encryption and a screen lock of five minutes or less. Report a lost or stolen
device to security@northwind.example within one hour.
